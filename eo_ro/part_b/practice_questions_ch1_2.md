# Practice Questions: Rajasthan Municipalities Act, 2009 (Chapters 1 & 2)

This document contains practice questions based on Chapter 1 (Preliminary) and Chapter 2 (Constitution and Government of Municipalities) of the Rajasthan Municipalities Act, 2009. These questions include PYQ concepts and are designed in the RPSC exam format.

---

### **Answers and Explanations**

**1. Answer: (B) Cantonment Areas**
*Explanation:* Section 1 clearly states that the Act applies to the whole of Rajasthan excluding Cantonment Areas.

**2. Answer: (D) 25 or more units**
*Explanation:* Section 2 defines a "Complex" as a commercial building with 25 or more units, or a residential building with 10 or more units.

**3. Answer: (B) 6 months**
*Explanation:* Under Section 3 (Delimitation), elections for a newly included area must be held within six months.

**4. Answer: (C) 13**
*Explanation:* Section 6 stipulates the minimum number of seats in any Municipality is 13.

**5. Answer: (B) 50%**
*Explanation:* Section 6 mandates a 50% reservation of all seats for women.

**6. Answer: (B) North-West corner**
*Explanation:* Section 10 states that the geographical numbering of wards must commence from the North-West corner.

**7. Answer: (C) State Election Commission**
*Explanation:* Section 11 vests electoral superintendence with the State Election Commission.

**8. Answer: (B) 1st day of January of the year the roll is prepared**
*Explanation:* Section 13 establishes this date as the qualifying date for voter registration.

**9. Answer: (B) 21 years**
*Explanation:* Section 21 lists 21 years as the minimum age for membership.

**10. Answer: (C) Imprisonment up to 1 year or a fine of ₹2,000 to ₹5,000, or both**
*Explanation:* This penalty is specified in Section 15.

**11. Answer: (A) Having a functional sanitary toilet at residence**
*Explanation:* Section 21 mandates that candidates must have a functional sanitary toilet at their residence.

**12. Answer: (C) They are deemed to have withdrawn from ALL seats and cannot contest**
*Explanation:* Section 22 clearly states that failing to withdraw extra nominations by the deadline results in withdrawal from all seats.

**13. Answer: (C) ₹2,000**
*Explanation:* Section 23 specifies a fine of up to ₹2,000 for such electoral violations.

**14. Answer: (B) 27th November 1995**
*Explanation:* Section 24 sets this exact cut-off date for the two-child norm disqualification.

**15. Answer: (B) Yes, persons under preventive detention are an exception and can vote**
*Explanation:* Section 25 generally prohibits prisoners from voting but explicitly makes an exception for those under preventive detention.

**16. Answer: (B) The State Government may choose to leave the seat unfilled until the next general election**
*Explanation:* Under Section 27, if the remaining term is less than 6 months, holding a bye-election is not mandatory.

**17. Answer: (C) No, Civil Courts have NO jurisdiction over these electoral matters**
*Explanation:* Section 30 explicitly bars Civil Courts from having jurisdiction over delimitation, seat allotments, and electoral rolls.

**18. Answer: (B) 1 month from the date of election**
*Explanation:* Section 31 states an election petition must be filed within 1 month.

**19. Answer: (B) District Judge**
*Explanation:* Section 31 requires the petition to be presented before the District Judge having territorial jurisdiction. 

**20. Answer: (C) High Court**
*Explanation:* Section 32 states that appeals from the District Judge's orders lie with the High Court.

**21. Answer: (B) Dissolve the Municipality**
*Explanation:* Section 33 mandates dissolution if more than 2/3rd of elections are declared void.

**22. Answer: (B) 1 month**
*Explanation:* Under Section 37, the oath must be taken within 1 month from the date of the first meeting.

**23. Answer: (C) 15 days**
*Explanation:* Section 38 states the resignation takes effect after 15 days from notice or upon acceptance, whichever is earlier.

**24. Answer: (B) 14 days**
*Explanation:* Section 42 requires MPs/MLAs to resign their former seat within 14 days to prevent ceasing to be a Municipal member.

**25. Answer: (B) 7 days**
*Explanation:* Section 49 makes it a duty of the CMO to inform the State Govt of illegal resolutions within 7 days.
